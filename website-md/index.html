What just happened?

The page carries the compiler — compiled from LocalGPT MD's Rust source to WebAssembly — and renders the result with the same viewer as localgpt.world. Sections become places along a winding path, a longer section raises a taller landmark, and a ```world fence in your file is honoured exactly. Nothing is sent anywhere; it works offline once loaded.

What the draft isn't: imagined scenery. That is the model's half — LocalGPT MD, the desktop app, authors each region with a local model and CC0 assets, and saves the result beside your file.

Worlds made in the app can be published for anyone to walk: a field guide · a deck · the gallery.

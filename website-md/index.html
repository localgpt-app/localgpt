What you get

  • Per scene, a .world package: the set (a room for INT., a location under a sun for EXT.), the cast as stand-ins, and the coverage as ext-cinematography cameras — a 24 mm master cropped to 2.39 and a 50 mm over on each speaker, aimed and focused at the actor by entity, so a revision that moves the actor keeps the shot.
  • A shot list (shotlist.csv): scene, shot, size, lens, sensor, aspect, camera height, distance to subject, in and out on the clock.
  • A printable board (board.html): one card per shot with its frame rendered by the Open World Format's reference viewer — the same file this page shows below.
  • The draft is deterministic and model-free: the same pages give the same board, byte for byte, here and in the localgpt-previs command line. Revising shots by asking — "make 1B a 35", "stay on her", a treatment as a branch — is the desktop app's half.

Final Draft files

  • A .fdx is read as Final Draft saved it: scene headings with their scene numbers, characters, parentheticals, dialogue (dual dialogue too), transitions, action. Acts become sections, script notes become notes, the cast list is dropped. The title page's first line is the title; a "written by" line and the one after it are the credit and the author.
  • Final Draft wraps action by its page layout, so the draft re-wraps it at a screenplay page's 61-character action column before timing it — the same page-time estimate a hard-wrapped Fountain file gets. Styling, revision marks and page breaks don't reach the board. The three samples above are the same screenplay three ways, and they draft to the same board, byte for byte.

Script pages in Markdown

  • A heading at any depth that reads as a scene heading — ## INT. KITCHEN - DAY, ### EXT. CLIFF PATH - DUSK — is that scene. Any other heading is an act or sequence.
  • A bold name with a colon opens dialogue: **MAYA:** You're late. A leading *(beat)* is the parenthetical. A plain MAYA: You're late. works when the name is uppercase.
  • Front matter (title:, author:, credit:) is the title page; failing that, the first # heading before any scene is the title. An HTML comment is a note.
  • Everything else is action, as written — and Fountain inside a .md file passes through untouched: an uppercase cue over its lines, a bare INT. line, > CUT TO:.

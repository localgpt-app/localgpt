A world you can open, change and rewind

One desktop app, one open format, and the agents you already use.

One native app

A Markdown document, a song or a .world package opens in the same Rust window. Write beside the world, play its soundtrack, adjust its shots and inspect its contents. The desktop app →

Your agent, beside it

Run your coding agent in a terminal next to the world, or connect it over MCP. It edits through the world's local API; LocalGPT supplies the tools, not a model, a login or another agent. Connect an agent →

History is the format

A .world holds the current scene, its assets and the log of every change. Undo, replay, compare branches and share the result through the Open World Format. Open a live canvas →

Previs without the setup

Drop a screenplay on localgpt.md for a board, a shot list and one world per scene. Nothing is uploaded. Open a scene in the app to revise its coverage and compare treatments. Previs workflow →

Build together

Host a Gen session on your network. Friends can walk the same world from their own window or a browser, while your agent makes the changes. Collaborative sessions →

Share outside the app

Open worlds in the browser at localgpt.world, export a self-contained HTML page, or take glTF into another 3D tool. Export options →

Welcome to the **LocalGPT** documentation: the [desktop app](/docs/app/) and its modes, [LocalGPT Gen](/docs/gen/) with the [live canvas](/docs/gen/live-canvas/) and [previs](/docs/gen/previs/), and [`localgpt world`](/docs/cli-commands/), a `.world` package's command line. Every app here reads and writes one format, the [Open World Format](https://openworldformat.org), an open standard with its own specification; the [architecture](/docs/architecture/) page says how the crates ride it.

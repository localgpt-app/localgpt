# A Walk Through LocalGPT MD

Every `##` section of this file becomes a place. Press → to walk to the next
one. Edit this file while the app is open and the world rebuilds.

## The Clearing

A ring of pale birches around a mossy clearing, fog hanging low at dusk.
A single lantern glows on a flat stone in the middle.

## The Library

Tall shelves spiral up into darkness. Ladders lean against dusty spines, and a
shaft of warm light cuts through the air where motes drift and settle.

- a reading desk with an open atlas
- a brass lamp that never quite goes out
- stacks of letters tied with string

## The Observatory

A brass telescope on a hilltop dome, pointed at a sky crowded with slow-turning
planets.

## The Shore

Black sand, a long wooden pier, and quiet waves that glow faintly blue where
they break.

Design note: asset ranking is driven by the track’s audio embedding only. The probe measured CLAP text↔text similarity as off-manifold (ocean→coral 0.18 < ocean→monolith 0.22 — noise) — the model is trained audio↔text, so recipe “intent” prose stays on the keyword path. Probe: ml::tests::text_embedding_probe.
